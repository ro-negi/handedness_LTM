Description - 

We studied how the likelihood of handedness (hand preference) change from simple to complex tasks. 

Mathematical operations - 

Handedness index (HI) = (right-left)/(right+left)

HI = mean = (right hand - left hand)/total number of events


Statistical analysis: Repeated Measures Correlation 

We computed a correlation between task complexity (gutt score) and handedness index. 


Results - 

r (rm corr)
0.6076312

degrees of freedom
102

p-value
7.943182e-12

95% confidence interval
0.4700493 0.716386
